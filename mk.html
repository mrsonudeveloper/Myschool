About Us

M.K Public School provides quality education for students from Nursery to Class 10. Our mission is to ensure the academic and moral development of every student. Our experienced teachers and modern facilities create an inspiring learning environment for students. Along with academics, the school also gives importance to sports, arts, and cultural activities. We are a community-centered institution that values the trust of parents and students. Our goal is to help every child achieve their dreams and build a bright future.
